## The Problem

FORVEX is a process-engineering, automation, and software venture aimed at SMBs in Córdoba. The planned sales channel is not cold traffic: it is a future talk with founders, where a link is shared. They needed a landing that welcomes that already-warm visitor, reinforces the message in a few seconds, and leads to a concrete CTA — book via form or continue on WhatsApp — without friction and without a custom domain yet.

## Solution and Architecture

I built the landing in Astro with strict TypeScript and native CSS: a light static site, clear typography and rhythm, and an information architecture designed for someone arriving from the conversation (operational friction, how they work, which industries fit, who they are). The close offers two equivalent paths: contact form or WhatsApp, so visitors can choose their preferred channel. The preview URL is already usable for sharing during the talk.

## Impact

There is a production landing at [forvex-landing-page.vercel.app](https://forvex-landing-page.vercel.app/) ready to use as a post-talk capture page. Visitors can validate the offer and get in touch without depending on a final domain. The model matches other portfolio cases: real published evidence, no invented metrics.
