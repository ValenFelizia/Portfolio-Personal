## The Problem

Felisa sold everything manually over WhatsApp. That worked, but the process became a bottleneck: a large part of the day went into answering catalog questions from people who only wanted to browse products and prices without buying. At the same time, ready-made e-commerce platforms were evaluated, but maintenance costs and per-sale commissions did not fit the business structure.

## Solution and Architecture

Instead of forcing a complex payment gateway, I built a web catalog where customers pick products and build a cart on the page. At checkout, the system generates and formats an order message ready to send on WhatsApp. The business owner still closes sales in the channel she already knew, but without repeating the same information in every conversation.

So she would not depend on me for every price or product change, I integrated a headless CMS (Sanity) that lets her manage catalog, prices, and stock without touching code. Skipping a ready-made storefront kept 0% commission per sale and adapted the flow to the real business.

## Impact

Today the catalog is centralized on the web and live in production: anyone who only wants to see products and prices can do so without messaging on WhatsApp. Orders arrive with the details already assembled, which reduces friction in daily support. The owner manages catalog, prices, and stock from the CMS without depending on code changes. The model stays commission-free, unlike the ready-made stores evaluated at the start. You can verify it on the [live catalog](https://felisa-bay.vercel.app/).
