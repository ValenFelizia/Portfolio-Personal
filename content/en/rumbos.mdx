## The Problem

787 Rumbos was already successful in its industry, but it had no official website. In travel, that matters: many travelers want to verify that an agency is real before they message or transfer money. Without a verifiable digital contact point, the business lost legitimacy with cautious clients in a market where scams are a real concern.

## Solution and Architecture

The goal was not an e-commerce store or a complex system: it was a clear, fast digital presentation. I designed a landing with a simple information architecture (services, contact, and brand presence), prioritizing load speed and a smooth mobile experience. That decision avoided overbuilding the project and focused on the concrete problem: creating trust at first contact.

## Impact

The agency now has an official production site at [787rumbos.com.ar](https://787rumbos.com.ar), with its own domain and verifiable contact details. Prospective clients can review services and brand presence before messaging — critical in a vertical where trust decides whether they move forward. The site did not replace the existing sales channel: it gave that channel visible institutional backup.
